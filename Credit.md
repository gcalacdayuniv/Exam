D. The managed currency operations under the Central Bank of the Philippines.

C. The Mercantilist Doctrine

**3. In 1918, a farmer receives a government loan for his crops but deliberately refuses to pay it back, viewing the funds as an absolute handout with no attached obligations. This behavior illustrates which specific cause of early credit failure?**

C. The Rehabilitation Finance Corporation
D. The Obras Pias Charitable Foundation

**5. A supplier ships millions of pesos worth of goods to a brand-new client based entirely on the client's flawless community track record and verbal integrity, without requiring any physical assets as backup. Which foundational element of credit is most heavily utilized here?**
A. The guarantee of legal obligation and enforcement.
B. The absolute reliance on trust and confidence.
C. The precise measurement of futurity and time.

**7. A lender feels uneasy about a tech startup's revenue projections and demands that the founders pledge their commercial real estate to offset potential default. What credit element is the lender attempting to manage?**

B. The inherent risk stretching over the life of the loan.

**20. A corporate auditor calculates that a manufacturing firm has PHP 50 million in total assets and PHP 30 million in total liabilities, yielding a net worth of PHP 20 million. Which specific 'C' of credit is the auditor evaluating?**
